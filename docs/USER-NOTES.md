## Living Isometric Hold

The Kingdom view features a classic isometric 2.5D pixel diorama (inspired by Realm Grinder).
- **Cobblestone Roads & Terrain**: Raised 3D stone cliff edge around the hold with paved paths.
- **Detailed Pixel Buildings**: Distinctive silhouettes for all buildings, animated chimney smoke, fluttering banners, construction scaffolding, and level upgrade pips.
- **Living Citizens (Walkers)**: 8 animated pixel citizens (villagers, woodcutters, miners, merchants, sentries, scholars) stroll between buildings or gather near the center square.
- **Building & Upgrading**: Hovering reveals an illuminated gold ground diamond. Clicking empty tiles places selected buildings; clicking existing buildings upgrades them up to level 5.

## Theme Packs & Holiday Overlays

The realm's atmosphere shifts across 9 distinct theme packs (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, All Hallows, Midwinter Tide, Dawn Feast, Harvest Moon, Midsummer Solstice).
- **Sticky TesterBar**: Use the pinned bar at the top to preview any holiday overlay instantly.
- **Recorded Audio & Synth Fallback**: Unmuting music plays `/audio/<id>.ogg` if present (including the official `halloween.ogg` loop). Missing tracks seamlessly play the procedural synth bed. Active wars trigger battle audio.
