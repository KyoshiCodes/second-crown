## Living Seasons and Holidays

- The realm now changes visually and acoustically with the passage of the 4 seasons:
  - **Spring**: Lush greens, blossoming petals, uplifting lydian chimes.
  - **Summer**: Golden meadows, fireflies, radiant warmth.
  - **Autumn**: Russet foliage, falling leaves, nostalgic dorian tones.
  - **Winter**: Snow-covered ground, rooftop snow drifts, crystalline bells.
- **Holidays**: Real-world calendar holidays (Halloween, Midwinter / Christmas, Dawn Feast / Easter, Harvest Moon, Midsummer Solstice) bathe the hold in unique lighting, animated particle wisps, and music cues.
- **Playtester Override**: Use the "Holiday overlay" dropdown at the top of the screen to preview any holiday at any time!
- **Spoils Bag & Bazaar**: Loot items (Iron Shards, War Banners, Ash Relics, Crown Splinters) now feature color-coded rarity chips (`common`, `uncommon`, `rare`, `epic`).
- **Army & Combat**: Royal Champion now has a custom portrait and battlefield victories/defeats play sound stingers.
