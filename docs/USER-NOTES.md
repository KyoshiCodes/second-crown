## Overworld Map

Zooming out to the regional board now reveals a full 3D kingdom landscape in Lords Mobile style:
- Elevated terrain with stepped vertical cliffs, granite rock faces, highland contour terraces, and glowing volcanic fissures.
- Holds on the board display authentic miniature pixel keeps tailored to each kingdom's culture (ashlar stone towers, timber longhouses, limestone courtyard keeps, felt yurts, stilt pile-houses, and iron-spiked battlements).
- Uncharted provinces are shrouded by towering volumetric cloud veils.
- Map canvas expands across the full width of your viewport rather than a restricted box.

## Keep gate

Kingdom tab now shows how high other works may go for each Keep level. Raise the Keep to unlock higher upgrades. Yard next to the Keep still feeds nearby works.

## Combat

Fights run in rounds. Fallen go to the Infirmary until beds are full. Treat on the War tab spends 4 food.
