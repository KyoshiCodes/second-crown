# Tutorial / Primer

Nine steps. Banner at the top of the shell. Skip is allowed.

1. Farm (Kingdom)
2. Cottage (Kingdom)
3. See the Board
4. Scout a far tile
5. Queue a company (Army)
6. March a column
7. Plant a flag or start a gather
8. Walls or Gate on the rim
9. Start a Crown study

Old saves: `tutorial_index` still works. New steps after the old six may reappear until skipped or finished.
